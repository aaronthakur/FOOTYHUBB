HOW IT STARTED

FROM A KIT BAG TO A FULL-TIME OBSESSION

FOOTYHUB began in 2019 when three friends got tired of paying markup for imported jerseys that never fit right. We started sourcing directly, testing every boot ourselves, and selling to the five-a-side league we played in on weekends.

Word spread fast. Today we ship match-day kits, training gear, and club merchandise to fans across the country, but the standard hasn't changed: if we wouldn't wear it on the pitch, we don't sell it.
